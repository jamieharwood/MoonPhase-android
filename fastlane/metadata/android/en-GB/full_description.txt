Orbitly shows tonight's Moon and what is happening in the sky above you, on your phone and your Wear OS watch. Everything astronomical is calculated on your device; live feeds add the aurora forecast and what is in orbit.

MOON
• Phase name, illumination and age, drawn as a moon with a live terminator
• Days to the next full moon and the current Earth–Moon distance

EARTH AND SUN
• Sunrise, sunset and daylight hours for your location
• Earth's orbital speed and axial tilt
• Countdown to the solstices, perihelion and aphelion

SKY CALENDAR
• Upcoming meteor showers with peak dates and Moon interference
• Solar and lunar eclipses to 2030
• A Sky calendar home-screen widget

AURORA
• NOAA planetary Kp index, highlighted during geomagnetic storms
• Optional notification when a storm (Kp 5 or more) is under way

SPACE
• ISS, Tiangong and Hubble altitudes from live orbital data
• Starlink and Kuiper satellite counts and the total number of tracked objects
• Who is in space right now, by spacecraft
• Distances to the planets with light travel time, and to Voyager 1 and 2, New Horizons, Hubble and James Webb

HISTORY
• Keeps a snapshot every 12 hours and charts any of 35 metrics over a week, a month, a year or all time
• Export the data as CSV

WEAR OS WATCH APP
• Moon, sun, aurora, ISS and more, sized for a round screen
• A Moon phase Tile with an illumination ring
• A watch-face complication showing illumination, days to full moon or the phase name
• Works on its own; the phone app sends it your location and units

ALSO
• Distances in AU, kilometres or miles
• Dark red night mode that protects your night vision
• Available in English, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese
• Material You colours
• Optional push to an Awtrix LED clock on your home network

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is typed in by you and never leaves your devices.
