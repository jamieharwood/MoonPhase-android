Orbitly Mission is a clean, mission-control style digital watch face for Wear OS, built around tonight's Moon.

FREE
• Large digital time, the date and UTC time
• The Moon drawn in its real phase, with the phase name, updated through the month
• A minimal always-on mode that saves battery

WITH ORBITLY PRO
Install the free Orbitly app on your phone and watch and unlock Orbitly Pro (a one-time purchase in the phone app) to fill the five complication slots with live astronomy:
• Moon illumination and days to the next full moon
• Aurora Kp index
• ISS altitude
• Sunrise and sunset for your location
Without Pro those slots stay empty, so the free face stays clean.

Made with Watch Face Format for Wear OS 5 and newer (Galaxy Watch4 and later, Pixel Watch and others). No ads, no tracking, no data collected.
